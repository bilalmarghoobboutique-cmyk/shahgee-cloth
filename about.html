About Us

Our story, our people, our promise

Since Generations

Serving Lahore Cantt with Quality and Trust

Shah Gee Cloth & Boutique House has been a trusted name in Main Bazaar #2, Ghaziabad, Lahore Cantt for many years. We take pride in offering premium quality fabrics and readymade garments for both ladies and gents.

Our collection includes summer and winter fabrics, traditional shawls, dhoti lacha, romals, and complete readymade kafan with accessories. We carefully handpick every piece to ensure our customers receive nothing but the best.

Whether you're looking for elegant everyday wear or something special for a memorable occasion, we have something for everyone.
